# 🎬 Unit 14: I was doing **and** I did
**⏱️ 20:00 → 25:00 (5 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

The cleverest unit in the whole past section: putting **both past tenses together**
in one sentence.

## 🧠 The big idea: long action + short action

| Tense | Job | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| **was doing** (past continuous) | the **long** background action | 🎥 the video playing |
| **did** (past simple) | the **short** action that interrupts it | ⚡ the photo *BANG!* |

> ## *I **was walking** home when it **started** to rain.*
>
> - *was walking* = the long action (going on for minutes) 🚶‍♀️
> - *started* = the short action that interrupted it ⚡

---

## 🧠 The two magic words

| Word | Goes with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **while** | the **long** action → **was doing** | ***While** I **was having** a bath, the phone rang.* |
| **when** | the **short** action → **did** | *I **was having** a bath **when** the phone **rang**.* |

💡 Both sentences describe the **same moment** — just with the focus in a different
place! 🤯

---

## 🧠 More examples (say them out loud 🗣️)

- *We **were watching** TV **when** the lights **went** out.* 💡
- ***While** she **was cooking**, she **dropped** the spoon.* 🥄
- *They **were playing** football **when** it **began** to rain.* ⚽🌧️
- *I **was crossing** the road **when** I **saw** my teacher.* 👀

Notice: the **short** action is always the interesting one — that's the
**interruption**! 🎯

---

## 🧠 Which one do I choose? (the 3-second test)

Ask yourself:

> **"Was it going on for a while, or did it just happen *pop*?"**

| Going on for a while 🔄 | Just happened, pop! ⚡ |
|---|---|
| was/were + **doing** | past simple (**did / went / saw**…) |
| *was sleeping, were eating, was raining* | *rang, fell, started, saw, broke* |

---

## 🧠 Bonus: two long actions at once

If **both** actions are long, use **was doing** for both:

- ***While** I **was doing** my homework, my brother **was playing** the piano.*
- *They **were talking** **while** we **were eating**.*

*(Two long actions = two past continuous verbs. No past simple needed!)*

---

## ⚠️ The mistakes to avoid

❌ *While I **walked** home, it **was starting** to rain.* ← backwards! 🔄
✅ *While I **was walking** home, it **started** to rain.* ✅

**The LONG action gets *was doing*. The SHORT interruption gets the plain past.**

❌ *When it **was starting** to rain, I was walking home.*
✅ *When it **started** to rain, I was walking home.* ✅

*Start* is a **pop!** action — so it takes past simple. ⚡
