# 📖 Irregular Verbs (Yesterday Forms)

Print this. Regular verbs add **-ed**; these famous ones change completely. Learn
**five a day** and you'll know them all in two weeks. 🧱

---

## 🎵 The rhyming families (easier to remember!)

| Family | Verbs |
|---|---|
| **-ought / -aught** | buy → bought · bring → brought · think → thought · teach → taught |
| **-ew** | fly → flew · know → knew · grow → grew · throw → threw |
| **-old / -old** | tell → told · sell → sold |
| **-ang / -ung** | sing → sang · ring → rang · swim → swam |
| **same word** | put · cut · hit · let · shut |

---

## ⚠️ Remember: after *did / didn't*, use the **TODAY** form!

- *Did you **go**?* ✅ (not *went*)
- *I didn't **eat** it.* ✅ (not *ate*)

**The "yesterday" form only appears when it is the ONLY past word in the
sentence.** 🏆
