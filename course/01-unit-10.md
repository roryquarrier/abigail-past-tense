# 🏖️ Unit 10: was / were
**⏱️ 0:00 → 5:00 (5 minutes)**

## 🎯 Goal

Learn the **past of *am / is / are***.

---

## 🧠 The rule (very short)

| Now (present) | Past | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I **am** | I **was** | *I **was** at the beach yesterday.* |
| he / she / it **is** | he / she / it **was** | *She **was** happy.* |
| you / we / they **are** | you / we / they **were** | *They **were** tired.* |

> ## 🎵 Say this rhythm:
> ## I was · he was · she was · it was
> ## you were · we were · they were

💡 **Trick:** *was* has just **one** letter change from *is* — and it's the
**singular** one. *Were* rhymes with *there* and goes with **more than one** (and
with *you*, even when *you* is one person! 😄).

## 🧠 Describing yesterday with was/were

Use *was/were* to **describe** — the weather, feelings, places, people:

- *Yesterday it **was** sunny.* ☀️
- *The water **was** cold!* 🌊
- *I **was** hungry after swimming.* 🍜
- *My friends **were** at the beach too.* 👫
- *They **were** very good at volleyball.* 🏐

---

## 🧠 Saying NO: wasn't / weren't

Just add **not**:

| ✅ Positive | ❌ Negative (short) |
|---|---|
| I was | I **wasn't** |
| She was | She **wasn't** |
| We were | We **weren't** |
| They were | They **weren't** |

- *I **wasn't** tired.*
- *There **weren't** any fish.* 🐟

---

## 🧠 Asking: Was …? Were …?

**Move the word to the front** — that's all:

| Telling | Asking |
|---|---|
| *You **were** at school.* | ***Were** you at school?* |
| *She **was** late.* | ***Was** she late?* |

**Short answers** (never say just "yes" or "no"!):

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| *Were you at school?* | *Yes, I **was**.* | *No, I **wasn't**.* |
| *Was she late?* | *Yes, she **was**.* | *No, she **wasn't**.* |

---

## ⚠️ The mistake to avoid

❌ *I **were** happy.* → ✅ *I **was** happy.*

❌ *They **was** at the park.* → ✅ *They **were** at the park.*

**One person (I, he, she, it) = was. More than one (we, they) = were.**
And remember: ***you* always takes *were*** — even for one person. 🙂

---

## 🧠 was/were + places

- *I **was** at home.* 🏠
- *She **was** in the garden.* 🌷
- *We **were** on the bus.* 🚌
- *They **were** at the cinema.* 🎬

**Careful!** *I was **at** home* (not "in home"). But *I was **in** the house* 🏠.
