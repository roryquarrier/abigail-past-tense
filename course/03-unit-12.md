# ⭐ Unit 12: I didn't … Did you … ?
**⏱️ 10:00 → 15:00 (5 minutes) — the big one!**

## 🎯 Goal

The unit nearly everyone gets wrong — so this one gets **extra time**, **its own
cheat sheet**, and the **longest quiz**. By the end you'll never write *"Did you
went"* again. 😎

---

## 🧠 The golden rule (learn this one sentence)

> ## 🏆 **When you use *did* / *didn't*, the next verb goes BACK to normal.**
>
> *didn't **go*** · *Did you **go**?* — **never** *didn't went*, **never** *Did you went*.

Why? Because **did** is already the past! Only **one** word needs to carry the
past — so the main verb stays in its **base form** (the dictionary form).

Think of it like this: 🏃‍♀️💨

> **did / didn't = the past-carrier.** Once it's carrying the past, the other verb
> can relax and wear its normal clothes. 👕

---

## 🧠 1. Saying NO: **didn't + base verb**

| ✅ What you did | ❌ What you didn't do |
|---|---|
| I **went** to school. | I **didn't go** to school. |
| She **played** tennis. | She **didn't play** tennis. |
| They **ate** the cake. | They **didn't eat** the cake. |

Notice: **went → go**, **played → play**, **ate → eat**.
**The past form disappears as soon as *didn't* arrives!** ✨

---

## 🧠 2. Asking: **Did + person + base verb … ?**

| Telling | Asking |
|---|---|
| *You **went** to school.* | ***Did** you **go** to school?* |
| *She **saw** the film.* | ***Did** she **see** the film?* |
| *They **finished** it.* | ***Did** they **finish** it?* |

## 🧠 3. Short answers (two parts, always!)

| Question | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
|---|---|---|
| *Did you **see** him?* | *Yes, I **did**.* | *No, I **didn't**.* |
| *Did she **like** it?* | *Yes, she **did**.* | *No, she **didn't**.* |

> ⚠️ **Never** answer with just *"Yes."* or *"No."* — always finish with
> **did / didn't**. That's how you show you understood the past tense! ✅

---

## 🧠 4. The BIG question: *did* or *was/were*?

This catches everybody. Ask: **is there a verb, or just a description?**

| If the sentence has… | Then the question is… |
|---|---|
| a normal verb (go, play, eat) | **Did** + person + verb? → *Did you **go**?* |
| **no other verb** — just be (was/were) | swap *was/were* to the front → ***Were** you tired?* |

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| *Did you **were** tired?* | ***Were** you tired?* |
| *Did she **was** at home?* | ***Was** she at home?* |
| *I didn't **was** tired.* | *I **wasn't** tired.* |

💡 **was/were never meet *did*.** They do their own job. 🚫

---

## 🧠 5. The three-sentence drill (do it out loud!)

For any verb, say all three forms:

> **I went.** → **I didn't go.** → **Did you go?**

Try it with: *play · eat · see · finish · have · make* 🗣️

*I played. I didn't play. Did you play?* ✅
*I had lunch. I didn't have lunch. Did you have lunch?* ✅

**Feel how the second and third sentences use the plain verb?** That's the whole
unit. You've got it. 🏆

---

## 🎯 Unit 12 in three lines

1. **didn't + base verb** → *I didn't **go***
2. **Did + person + base verb** → *Did you **go**?*
3. **Short answers need did/didn't** → *Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**.*
