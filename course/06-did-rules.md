# ⭐ The *did* Rules (Unit 12 Cheat Sheet)

Print this page. It fixes the most common mistake in the whole past tense. 🖨️

---

## 🏆 The golden rule

> **One past only!**
> *did / didn't* is **already past** — so the next verb goes back to its
> **base form**.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I didn't **went** | I didn't **go** | *didn't* = past already |
| Did you **saw** it? | Did you **see** it? | *did* = past already |
| She didn't **ate** | She didn't **eat** | same rule |
| Did they **finished**? | Did they **finish**? | same rule |

---

## 📋 The three forms of any verb

| | Form |
|---|---|
| ➕ Telling | I **went** |
| ➖ Saying no | I **didn't go** |
| ❓ Asking | **Did** you **go**? |

Practise with *eat, see, play, have, take, write, make, buy*:

> *I **had** breakfast. → I **didn't have** breakfast. → **Did** you **have** breakfast?* ✅

---

## 🗣️ Short answers (always two parts!)

| Question | ✅ | ❌ |
|---|---|---|
| Did you like it? | Yes, I **did**. | No, I **didn't**. |
| Did she call? | Yes, she **did**. | No, she **didn't**. |

---

## 🚫 *did* and *was/were* never work together

| Situation | Correct question |
|---|---|
| There's a **verb** (go, play, eat) | **Did** you **go**? |
| There's **only be** (was/were) | **Were** you tired? |

❌ *Did you were tired?* ❌ *Did she was home?* ❌ *I didn't was happy.*

✅ *Were you tired?* ✅ *Was she home?* ✅ *I wasn't happy.*

---

## 🔤 Word order (the sentence skeleton)

> **Did + person + base verb + the rest + ?**

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| Did | **Did** |
| person | **you** |
| base verb | **finish** |
| rest | your homework |
| | → ***Did you finish your homework?*** |

**Negative skeleton:** **person + didn't + base verb**
→ *She **didn't finish** her homework.* ✅

---

## 🧪 30-second self-test

Say all three out loud for *"see a film"* 🎬:

*I **saw** a film. → I **didn't see** a film. → **Did** you **see** a film?*

If the middle and last ones used **see** (not *saw*) — you've mastered Unit 12. 🏆
