# 🏃 Unit 13: I was doing (Past Continuous)

## 🧠 The rule

> ## **was / were + verb-ing**

| Person | Past continuous |
|---|---|
| I | I **was playing** |
| he / she / it | She **was reading** |
| you / we / they | We **were watching** |

**Same *was/were* rule as Unit 10** — you already know that part! Then just add
**-ing**. 🎉

---

## 🧠 When do we use it?

When an action was **still going on** at a moment in the past:

- *At 8 o'clock last night I **was doing** my homework.* 📚
- *This time yesterday we **were flying** to Japan.* ✈️
- *When you called, I **was having** a shower.* 🚿

💡 **Photo vs video:**
- *I **swam**.* = one photo, finished 📸 (past simple)
- *I **was swimming** at 3 o'clock.* = a video clip, still playing 🎬 (past continuous)

---

## 🧠 The spelling of **-ing** (3 rules)

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Most verbs: add **-ing** | play → play**ing** |
| Verb ends in **e**: drop the e | write → writ**ing** · make → mak**ing** |
| Short verb (consonant + vowel + consonant): **double** the last letter | run → ru**nn**ing · sit → si**tt**ing · swim → swi**mm**ing |

**Careful:** *lie → lying*, *die → dying* (they're special! 🙂)

---

## 🧠 Saying NO and asking

**Negative:** *wasn't / weren't + verb-ing*
- *I **wasn't** sleeping. I was reading.* 👀
- *They **weren't** listening.*

**Question:** move *was/were* to the front (like Unit 10!)
- ***Was** she sleeping?*
- ***Were** you waiting for me?*
- *Yes, I **was**. / No, I **wasn't**.*

⚠️ Notice: **no *did* here either!** *Was* and *were* do the job on their own.
❌ *Did you were sleeping?* ✅ *Were you sleeping?*

---

## 🧠 The time words that love the past continuous

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| **at 7 o'clock** | *At 7 o'clock I **was eating** breakfast.* |
| **this time yesterday** | *This time yesterday I **was sitting** in class.* |
| **while** | *While I **was reading**, he was drawing.* |
| **all morning / all day** | *It **was raining** all morning.* ☔ |

---

## ⚠️ The mistake to avoid

❌ *I **was** play football.* → ✅ *I **was playing** football.*
*(was needs the -ing! They always go together. 👫)*

❌ *I **playing** football.* → ✅ *I **was playing** football.*

**was/were + doing** — like shoes and socks. Always both! 🧦👟
